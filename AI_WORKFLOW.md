# AI Insight Workflow

## Objective
Turn validated financial metrics into concise management insights without allowing AI to invent financial facts.

## Prompt pattern

You are a finance analyst reviewing validated financial metrics.

Using ONLY the supplied figures:
1. Identify departments with material budget overspend.
2. Quantify the variance.
3. Highlight positive performance where relevant.
4. Suggest questions management should investigate.
5. Do not invent causes that are not present in the data.
6. Clearly distinguish observed facts from hypotheses.
7. Recommend human review before any financial decision.

## Human-in-the-loop principle
AI is used to summarize and structure insights, not to replace financial judgment or controls.
